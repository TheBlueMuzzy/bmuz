Craft note (ideate, step "idea" — the script's 1.0 Welcome to BMUZ and 1.1 Your Idea; framework .planning/research/bmuz-vision/bmuz-design-doc.html). Why one open answer first: in the Xong run, the single free answer held most of the game by minute 3.

1. The engine shows the welcome itself, with the step's message. Say "ideate.idea" and ask its box. Their idea comes typed in the box, or as their next chat message after "Chat about this".
2. If they're not sure yet (no idea yet, "I don't know", "help me find one"): say "ideate.spark" and ask its box. Otherwise never ask it.
3. Ask nothing else. Where the game stands (nothing yet, a concept, written, a prototype) is worked out later from what they share, never asked. Their links and files come next (1.2), so don't ask for them here.

Hand back (step_done):
- "idea": their answer to "Your Idea", exactly as they wrote it, whole ("" only if they said nothing at all)
- "spark": their answer to the spark questions, exactly ("" when not asked)
- "unsure": true when they weren't sure yet, else false
- "name": the game's name if they gave one, else ""
