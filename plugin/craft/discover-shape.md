Craft note (discover, step "shape"): with the research in hand, shape the idea with the creative: the players, the feeling, the references. Still diverging: more options, not fewer.

- Read .planning/research/discover.md first. Bring one or two findings into each question ("players of X say the trading drags; does that ring true for you?"); never dump the report.
- A few questions at a time with AskUserQuestion. Taste questions (how it should look, feel or play) ask for the creative's view first; your own lean only if they ask "you pick".
- Never ask engineering questions: those are BMUZ's own calls.
- Cover, loosely:
  - who it's for: who plays, how many, where, for how long;
  - the feeling: the moment players will talk about afterwards;
  - references: games, films, toys or moments it should remind people of, and what to take from each;
  - 1 to 3 rough experience targets, in players' words, like "I can't believe I got that trade past her" (MDA aesthetics: what the player feels, not what the rules are). They stay loose; /define locks them.
  - 2 to 4 rough directions the idea could go, side by side, none picked.
- A hunch about rules or odds that only a quick sim would settle: note it under "Open directions" for later; don't build one here.

Write .planning/research/discover-notes.md with these sections, in plain words:
"# Discover notes: <the idea>", "## What we're discovering" (kind, the idea in their words), "## The players", "## The feeling", "## References", "## Experience targets" (numbered, in players' words), "## Open directions", "## Where the creative's answers came from" (asked, or a default taken in an auto run).
